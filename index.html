ABOUT

Engineering with a security mindset.

My background spans addiction counseling, public health, and operations management—work that demanded clear documentation, confidentiality, careful judgment, and practical problem-solving. I bring those habits to software engineering and cybersecurity.

I build full-stack applications with Python, FastAPI, SQL, and LLM integrations. Alongside that development work, I’m expanding into application security: testing my own applications, examining authentication and authorization boundaries, reviewing findings, and documenting remediation.

I’m especially interested in the overlap between AI engineering and security: building useful AI-enabled systems while protecting the data, users, and tools they depend on.
